## Website Purpose

The public website exists to:

- document the journey towards consistent futures trading
- share trading videos, journal entries, lessons and progress
- build trust through honest content
- introduce visitors to Playbit

## Brand Position

Degenius Liquidity documents the real process of building a sustainable futures trading business.

The public story focuses on:

- trading progress
- funded accounts and evaluations
- payouts
- wins and losses
- trading psychology
- lessons learned
- content creation
- building the website and social media presence
- behind-the-scenes business development

The public story must not reveal:

- why previous employment ended
- personal debt or private financial circumstances
- real identity
- face
- home address or precise location
- family details
- other identifying personal information

Privacy should be respected without making anonymity a prominent part of the brand.

---

## Brand Promise

## Journal Requirements

The journal must be quick to update.

The long-term goal is for objective trade data to be captured automatically.

For the current website, manual input should be kept minimal.

A journal entry should require only:

- date
- market
- model
- result
- a few natural sentences about the session

The system should derive or generate where practical:

- title
- summary
- lesson
- reading time
- tags
- slug
- featured status

Product rule:

> Automate facts. Capture judgement. Learn from both.

## The Dashboard

The Dashboard is a future private application for running the Degenius Liquidity business.

It is not currently active development.

Its core purpose is:

> Automatically collect important data and turn it into something usable.

A stronger product statement is:

> The Dashboard automatically captures the facts, asks only for the judgement that matters, and turns both into insights that support better trading and business decisions.

Long-term possibilities include:

- automatic trade imports from TradingThings.io
- trade journaling
- psychology notes
- challenge tracking
- content tracking
- website publishing
- business income and expense tracking
- affiliate tracking
- project tracking
- voice input
- an AI assistant similar to Jarvis
- reports and useful patterns

The Dashboard should ask as few questions as possible.

It should not become another admin burden.
